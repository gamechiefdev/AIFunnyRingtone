Five designer skills applied

  1. Masonry rhythmPins of mixed heights create the Pinterest waterfall instead of a rigid grid.
  2. Soft elevation24–26dp radii, 1dp hairline strokes and near-zero shadows replace hard borders.
  3. Typographic scaleOne family (Poppins) across 6 sizes, tighter tracking on display text.
  4. Warm neutral canvas#FBF9F6 surfaces let the green brand and coral accents breathe.
  5. Micro-interactionRipple cards, a sliding nav indicator and a save affordance on every pin.
